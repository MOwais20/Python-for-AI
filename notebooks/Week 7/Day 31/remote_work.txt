Remote Work

Employees can work from home 2 days in one week. Ask your manager for the days you want.

You must be online from 10 am to 4 pm on a remote day. You must reply to messages within 30 minutes.

Use the company VPN when you work from home. Never use public wifi for company work.

The company pays Rs 3000 each month for your home internet. Send a photo of your internet bill to claim it.
