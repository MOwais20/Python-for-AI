Working Hours

The office is open from 9 am to 5 pm, Monday to Friday. The office is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

The lunch break is 1 hour, from 1 pm to 2 pm. Please do not book meetings in this time.

If you arrive after 9:30 am, you are marked late. Three late marks in one month cut half a day of salary.

Work after 5 pm is overtime. Overtime needs approval from your manager and is paid at 1.5 times the normal rate.
