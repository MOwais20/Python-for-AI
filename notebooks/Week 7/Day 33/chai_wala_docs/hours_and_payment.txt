Shop Timings and Payment

Our shop is on Main Boulevard, Gulberg, Lahore.

We are open every day from 9 am to 11 pm. On Friday we close from 1 pm to 2 pm for Jumma prayer.

On the first day of Eid we open at 2 pm. On the second and third day of Eid we open at the normal time.

You can pay with cash, card, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Cash on delivery is available. We do not accept cheques.
