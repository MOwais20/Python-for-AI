Complaints and Refund Policy

If your chai is cold or your order is wrong, send a photo on WhatsApp at 0300-1234567 within 1 hour of delivery.

We will send a new order for free, or give your money back. You can choose.

After 1 hour we cannot give a refund, but you can still share your feedback with us.

If a rider is rude, please send the order number and the time of delivery. The manager will call you back within one day.
